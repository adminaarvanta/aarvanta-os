# Marketing strategy — AI Team on Free

**Goal:** Drive Free signups into `/workforce` activation, then convert to paid when they need more AI employees, credits, or production channels.

## Positioning

- **Hook:** Hire your first AI employee free  
- **Proof:** Works with your CRM + Knowledge Hub (grounded answers — no bluffing)  
- **CTA:** Start free → AI Team  

## In-product (shipped)

1. Sidebar **AI Team** highlight + “Free” badge on Free plan  
2. Home dashboard promo card → `/workforce`  
3. Free tour step covering AI Team  
4. Explore banner with usage meters on AI Team / WhatsApp / Voice / People  

## Paid channels

### LinkedIn (primary B2B)

| Variant | Primary text | CTA |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| A — Outcome | Stop hiring for work an AI employee can draft today. Aarvanta Free includes 1 AI Team seat grounded in your docs. | Start free |
| B — Founder | Solo founders: get a Sales / Ops AI teammate without a salary. Free forever to start. | Open AI Team |
| C — Anti-bluff | Tired of chatbots inventing CRM deals? Ours answers from your Knowledge Hub — or admits when it doesn’t know. | Try Aarvanta Free |

### Meta / Instagram

Short creative: navy + gold still of AI Team UI. Caption: “Your first AI employee is free. Build the rest of the company on one OS.” CTA: Learn more → `/register`.

### Email nurture (Free signups, never opened `/workforce`)

1. Day 0: Welcome + “Meet your AI Team” deep link  
2. Day 3: “Upload one SOP → ask AI Team a real question”  
3. Day 7: Soft upgrade — more AI employees + WhatsApp/Voice go-live  

## Success metrics

- % Free users who open `/workforce` within 7 days  
- % who upload ≥1 Knowledge doc before first AI run  
- Free → paid conversion among AI Team actives vs non-actives
