## Usage notes

- Samples are **1024×1024** PNG (upscale to 1080×1080 for upload if needed).
- **01–05:** stick to navy/gold for always-on brand consistency.
- **06–10:** exploratory campaign colorways — keep Aarvanta lockup legible; don’t replace the logo with a recolored fake mark.
- Leave safe margins (~80–120px) for platform chrome.
- Do not invent dollar offers or ROI claims.
- Avoid third-party trademark logos in final paid creatives (product names in copy are fine).
- Logo source files: `public/aarvanta-logo-*.png` and `public/aarvanta-logo-mark-*.png`.
