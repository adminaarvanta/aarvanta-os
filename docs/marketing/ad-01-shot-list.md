## Split-screen spec (shots 9–10)

```
┌─────────────────────┐
│  AI specialist      │  ~45% height
│  + soft waveform    │
├─────────────────────┤
│  Human + phone      │  ~55% height
│  (or cut to full UI)│
└─────────────────────┘
```

- Hard horizontal divide or soft gradient bar in navy  
- Both halves 9:16-safe composition (faces not cut by TikTok UI)  
- Optional: after 4s, bottom half expands to full-bleed UI montage

---

## UI capture checklist (shot 10)

Record on staging / demo tenant in **light mode** for clarity on mobile video:

1. `/workforce` (AI Team Chat) — goal/prompt → visible plan card  
2. Jobs list with 2–3 realistic job titles  
3. Waiting for You — one approval item  
4. CRM — People or Sales (narrative, not empty)  
5. Communication hub / WhatsApp-style thread — polite business follow-up  

Export: 1080×1920 or crop cleanly from desktop with phone chrome mock.
